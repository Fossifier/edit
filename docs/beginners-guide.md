### Adblocking

> How important is using an adblocker?

Sites generally contain ads, some of which can be harmful, often leading to unwanted pages or fake download buttons. While real download buttons are usually small, often text-based or subtly integrated into the site's design, fake ones are frequently large, brightly colored, use generic terms like 'Download Now', and may appear multiple times on a page. These usually redirect to fake malicious download pages. Read this **[guide](https://cs.rin.ru/forum/viewtopic.php?f=14&t=159345)** to learn what these pages look like and keep in mind that real downloads happen within the download pages themselves, not redirected pages or popup tabs. However, it can be hard to tell them apart, which is why we strongly recommend the use of an **[adblocker](https://fmhy.net/privacy#adblocking)**.
You can also use a **[Redirect Skipper](https://fmhy.net/internet-tools#redirect-bypass)** to skip annoying countdowns. 

!!!note Using several adblockers, like uBO and AdGuard, at the same time can **[mess things up](https://x.com/gorhill/status/1033706103782170625)**. This only happens when combining regular universal adblockers with each other, not with specialized ones, so it's perfectly okay to use uBO alongside something like SponsorBlock.

### Antivirus

> How can I safely scan files and determine if detections are false positives?



Sometimes adblockers can fail, so before trusting any file, it's recommended to scan it through an **[Online Scanner](https://fmhy.net/privacy#online-scanners)** and run it in a **[sandbox](https://fmhy.net/privacy#sandboxes-vms)** first. If you're having trouble determining if something is a false positive, refer to this **[Scan Guide](https://clarasguide.valeena.workers.dev/Guides/vtguide/)**, or send it to us in our **[Discord](https://github.com/fmhy/FMHY/wiki/FMHY-Discord)** and we'll take a look for you. 

!!!note Most antivirus programs are unnecessary and can cause slowdowns. If you use trusted websites, Windows Defender should be all you need to stay safe, and you can run a scan with a **[trusted antivirus](https://fmhy.net/privacy#antivirus)** from time to time for extra protection.

### Torrenting

> Do I need a VPN to torrent?

Downloading files through torrenting can cause issues with your ISP, so using a **[VPN](https://fmhy.net/privacy#vpn)** is a must in most countries. You should also **[bind your VPN](https://wispydocs.pages.dev/torrenting/)** to your torrent client. This ensures that if your VPN connection drops, your torrent client stops all traffic, preventing your real IP address from being exposed. You can also use **[Remote Torrenting Services](https://fmhy.net/torrenting#remote-torrenting)** to avoid the need for a VPN altogether.


> What is Port Forwarding? Do I need it?

**[Port forwarding](https://wispydocs.pages.dev/torrenting/#port-forwarding)** (having an open port) is recommended for users torrenting without a VPN, or those with a VPN that supports port forwarding. While it's not strictly needed, it benefits the swarm, can sometimes boost download speeds, and improves connectivity for torrents with fewer seeds.

### Base64

> How do I decode Base64?

Strings of text that look like `aHR0cHM6Ly9mbWh5Lm5ldC8` are Base64 encoded, which we do to bypass automated takedowns. You can use a **[Base64 decoder](https://fmhy.net/text-tools#encode-decode)** to view them. Some may need to be decoded twice.

### Privacy

> I don't have anything to hide, why should I care about privacy?

Privacy is not about keeping secrets, but autonomy over your boundaries, security and opportunities by preventing your data from being used against you. Companies track your behavior, target ads and prices, and influence your choices. Privacy is a basic form of digital hygiene, the same way you close your curtains.

!!!note Never enter your real info on any site you don't know you can trust. Use a unique password for each site you register on. That way, if a breach occurs, only that one site's credentials are compromised. You can also take advantage of **[email aliasing](https://fmhy.net/internet-tools#email-aliasing)** for an extra layer of protection.

!!!note Keep in mind that it's **never** a good idea to upload personal or confidential files to any file host / cloud AI, even if they claim to be encrypted.
